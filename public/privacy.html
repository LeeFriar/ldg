Your information

Privacy notice

Last updated: 20 August 2026

In short: the website does not contain a contact form or analytics. If you contact LDG Electrical by email or telephone, the information you provide is used to respond, prepare quotations, carry out electrical work and meet legal obligations.

Who is responsible for your information?

Lloyd Garland, trading as LDG Electrical, is the controller responsible for deciding how and why personal information is used.

Email: lloyd@ldg-electrical.co.uk
Telephone: 07587 869215

Information we may collect

Depending on your enquiry or the work requested, this may include:

  • Your name and contact details.
  • The address where work is required and access arrangements.
  • Details of your enquiry, electrical requirements and correspondence.
  • Quotations, appointments, job records, photographs relevant to the work, invoices and payment records.
  • Information needed to comply with electrical safety, accounting, tax, insurance or legal requirements.
  • Limited technical information processed by website hosting infrastructure for security and reliable delivery.

Customers invited to leave feedback can submit a star rating and written comment without providing a name, email address or other identifier. Feedback should not include personal information. Approved project photographs and anonymous feedback may be displayed publicly on the website.

Information normally comes directly from you by telephone or email. It may also come from someone acting for you, a landlord, managing agent, main contractor or another person involved in the work.

Why we use it and our lawful bases

Enquiries and quotations

To answer questions, arrange visits and provide quotations. The lawful bases are taking steps at your request before entering a contract and, where appropriate, our legitimate interest in responding to business enquiries.

Providing electrical services

To schedule, manage, complete and communicate about agreed work. The lawful basis is performance of a contract with you.

Safety, certification, accounts and tax

To maintain appropriate work, financial and compliance records. The lawful bases are legal obligation and performance of a contract.

Business administration and legal claims

To manage payments, resolve complaints, protect the business and establish or defend legal claims. The lawful basis is our legitimate interest in operating and protecting the business fairly.

Project gallery and anonymous feedback

To show examples of completed work and genuine customer experiences. Project images are published only where appropriate, and customers receive a randomly generated single-use feedback link. Feedback does not request identity or contact details. The lawful basis is our legitimate interest in presenting completed work and customer experiences, balanced against privacy rights.

LDG Electrical does not use your information for automated decision-making or profiling and does not sell personal information.

Who we may share information with

Information is shared only when necessary. Recipients may include email, IT and hosting providers; accountants, insurers and professional advisers; suppliers, subcontractors or certification bodies involved in the work; payment providers or banks; and public authorities where disclosure is legally required.

Email hosting through OVHcloud

LDG Electrical uses OVHcloud as its email-service provider. Emails and associated account data are stored using OVHcloud’s Zimbra service in France, within the European Economic Area (EEA). OVHcloud processes this information on behalf of LDG Electrical to provide email hosting, delivery, security, spam filtering and related support services.

Sending this information from the UK to France is covered by the UK’s adequacy regulations for EEA countries. These regulations recognise an adequate level of protection for personal information transferred to the EEA.

Providers are expected to protect information and use it only for the relevant service. If a provider processes information outside the UK, an appropriate UK data-protection safeguard will be required.

How long we keep it

  • Enquiries that do not lead to work: normally up to 12 months after the last contact.
  • Quotations, customer records, job information, certificates and correspondence: normally up to six years after the work or relationship ends.
  • Invoices, payments and accounting records: for the period required by tax and accounting law, normally at least five years after the relevant filing deadline.
  • Information connected with a complaint, insurance matter or legal claim: for as long as reasonably needed to resolve and document it.

Information may be kept longer where required by law or where necessary for an active dispute. When no longer required, it will be securely deleted or destroyed.

How we protect information

Reasonable technical and organisational measures are used to protect information from loss, unauthorised access, alteration or disclosure. Access is limited to people and service providers who need it for the purposes described above.

Your data-protection rights

Depending on the circumstances and lawful basis, you may have rights to access your information, correct inaccurate information, request deletion or restriction, object to processing, and receive certain information in a portable form. These rights are not absolute and legal record-keeping requirements may apply.

Your right to object

You have the right to object to processing based on legitimate interests. Contact Lloyd using the details above and explain your concern.

To exercise a right, email lloyd@ldg-electrical.co.uk. You will not normally be charged, and identity may need to be confirmed before information is disclosed.

Complaints

Please contact LDG Electrical first so the concern can be addressed. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO), the UK data-protection regulator:

ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint
Telephone: 0303 123 1113

Changes to this notice

This notice will be reviewed periodically and updated when business practices or legal requirements change. The latest version will remain available on this page.

Website technology

Cookie notice

Last updated: 20 August 2026

The LDG Electrical website does not set cookies and does not use analytics, advertising trackers, browser storage or profiling technologies.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small files saved on a computer, phone or other device when a website is visited. Similar technologies can also store or access information on a device. Some are essential for a requested service; others are used for analytics, personalisation or advertising.

How this website operates

This is a static informational website. It does not provide user accounts, accept payments, submit contact forms or remember preferences. Telephone and email links open the relevant application on your device; they do not send information through this website.

The site does not use Google Analytics, advertising pixels or externally hosted web fonts. As no cookies or similar storage technologies are used, a cookie-consent banner is not displayed.

Hosting and security

The website hosting and network infrastructure necessarily processes technical connection information, such as an IP address, to deliver pages and maintain security. The LDG website’s web server is configured not to retain routine access logs. Infrastructure outside the website’s control, such as a reverse proxy or hosting platform, may process limited connection data for security and service delivery.

If this changes

If optional analytics, embedded third-party services or other non-essential technologies are introduced, this notice will be updated and an appropriate consent mechanism will be provided before those technologies are used.

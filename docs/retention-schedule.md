# LDG Electrical personal-data retention schedule

Last reviewed: 20 August 2026

| Record | Normal retention | Disposal/review |
|---|---:|---|
| Enquiries that do not lead to work | 12 months after last contact | Delete email and notes unless needed for a dispute |
| Quotations and related correspondence | 6 years after the quote or completed work | Secure deletion/destruction |
| Customer, job, certification and work records | 6 years after completion | Review for any continuing safety, certification, insurance or legal need |
| Invoices, expenses and payment records | At least 5 years after the relevant 31 January tax filing deadline | Retain longer if instructed by HMRC or an accountant |
| Complaints, incidents, insurance matters and legal claims | Until resolved, then normally 6 years | Review with insurer or adviser before deletion |
| Supplier and subcontractor records | 6 years after the relationship or transaction ends | Secure deletion/destruction |
| Data-rights requests and responses | 3 years after closure | Secure deletion |
| Personal-data breach records | 6 years after closure | Secure deletion |

Retention may be extended where required by law, an insurer, an active investigation or a legal claim. Records must not be kept merely because storage is available. Lloyd should review retained records annually and document exceptions.
